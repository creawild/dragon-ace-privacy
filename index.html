Dragon Ace Privacy Policy

Effective Date:
October 21, 2026
Privacy Contact:

This Privacy Policy explains how Dragon Ace (“the App”) processes user data. Dragon Ace is a VR dragon-riding game for Meta Quest.

This policy covers the current local gameplay features and the planned Meta account and global leaderboard features. The corresponding online data processing will begin only when those features become available.

1. Data We Process and Why

Meta Account Information

With the required platform access permissions, the App will attempt to retrieve the current player’s Meta User ID and available username or display name at startup. We use this information to:

  • Identify the current player.
  • Associate leaderboard results with the correct platform account.
  • Retrieve personal best results and rankings.
  • Display player names in leaderboards.

The App does not ask players to provide their Meta account passwords to us. User IDs link results to accounts but are not displayed as visible text in the leaderboard interface.

Game Data

The App processes level progress, game settings, scores, completion times, star ratings, and personal best results. This data supports saved progress, result screens, and leaderboards.

The App does not upload button-action logs, flight paths, or other step-by-step gameplay records for leaderboard functionality.

VR Interaction Data

The App processes the headset and controller tracking data and inputs needed for gameplay in real time on the device. This processing supports the VR view and game interactions. This data is not included in leaderboard submissions.

Contact Emails

When you contact us by email, we process the email address, message content, and information you voluntarily provide that is necessary to handle your request. We use this information to respond, verify identity, and handle data requests.

2. Leaderboard Submissions and Public Information

In versions that provide online leaderboards, the App automatically submits the result for the relevant level and difficulty after successful completion, when platform identity and services are available. There is no first-use confirmation dialog for enabling leaderboard submissions.

Higher scores rank first. For equal scores, shorter completion times rank first.

Player names, scores, completion times, and rankings displayed in a leaderboard are visible to other players who view it. Platform User IDs are not displayed as visible text.

3. Data Storage and Recipients

Game progress and settings are stored on the user’s device. If Meta Cloud Backup is enabled for the App, relevant saves may also be backed up by Meta.

Data required for account identification and leaderboard features is processed through Meta platform services. We do not operate our own servers to receive this game data. We do not use third-party advertising, analytics, or crash-reporting services to process this data.

Emails sent to our contact address are processed by the email service provider. Please provide only the information needed to handle your request.

Meta’s processing of platform data is also subject to its Privacy Policy and applicable platform terms.

4. Data Retention

Local game data remains until it is deleted or overwritten. Meta Cloud Backup is managed separately from local saves. Deleting local data or uninstalling the App does not mean that cloud backups or leaderboard records have been deleted.

Leaderboard records have no fixed automatic expiration period. We retain them only as needed to provide the relevant features. We will process deletion when records are no longer needed or after receiving and verifying an applicable deletion request.

Contact emails and request-handling records are retained only as needed to complete the request, resolve related disputes, or meet applicable legal obligations.

5. Deletion and Other Data Requests

Users in all countries and regions can request deletion of their personal data, free of charge, by emailing ronliu666@gmail.com. You can also contact us about our data practices or request access or correction under applicable law.

Please describe the scope of your request, such as a result for one level or all leaderboard records. We may take reasonable steps to verify your identity to prevent unauthorized deletion.

  • Local data: You can clear it through the device’s application data management features, where supported by the device system.
  • Leaderboard records: After verifying your identity, we will use Meta’s management APIs to process deletion of the relevant records and confirm the outcome.
  • Cloud backups: You can manage or delete relevant backups through Meta’s cloud backup management features.

If an applicable legal obligation or another lawful reason prevents us from fulfilling all or part of a request, we will explain why. We will provide guidance where a request requires a separate process through Meta.

Deleting existing leaderboard records does not permanently disable submissions. If you continue playing and complete a new run, a new result will be submitted when online features are available.

Do not send passwords, access tokens, or app secrets by email.

6. Data Security

We use security measures appropriate to the data we process. These include restricting access to administrative credentials, keeping app secrets out of the game client, and reasonably verifying identity before processing deletion requests.

No technical measure can guarantee absolute security. Please contact us if you discover a data security issue related to the App.

7. Age Eligibility

The App is intended for teenagers and adults aged 13 and older, subject to applicable age requirements in the user’s region.

If you believe that someone below the applicable age has provided personal data through the App, please contact us so we can investigate and take appropriate action.

8. Policy Changes and Contact

We may update this policy to reflect changes to the App or legal requirements. We will update the effective date on this page. Where separate notice or consent is required, we will follow the applicable requirements.
