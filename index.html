This Privacy Policy explains how Dragon Ace (“the App”) processes user data. Dragon Ace is a VR dragon-riding game for Meta Quest.

1. Data We Process and Why

Meta Account Information

With the required platform permissions, the App uses the Meta User ID and platform-provided username or display name to:

  • Identify the currently signed-in player.
  • Associate leaderboard results with the correct platform account.
  • Retrieve the player’s personal best results and rankings.
  • Display player names in the in-game leaderboards.

The User ID links results to the correct account. The App does not display this identifier as visible text in the leaderboard interface.

Game Data

The App processes level progress, game settings, scores, completion times, star ratings, and personal best results. This data supports saved progress, result screens, and leaderboard features.

VR Interaction Data

The App processes headset and controller positions, orientations, and input to display the VR view and support gameplay interactions.

2. Information Visible in Leaderboards

The App uses Meta platform services to provide global leaderboards.

Results submitted to a leaderboard are associated with the player’s platform identity. Player names, scores, completion times, and rankings displayed in a leaderboard are visible to other players who view that leaderboard.

3. Data Storage and Recipients

Game progress and settings are stored on the user’s device. Data needed for account identification and leaderboard features is processed through Meta platform services.

We do not currently operate our own servers to store this user data. We do not use third-party advertising, analytics, or crash-reporting services to process this data.

Meta’s processing of relevant data is also subject to its privacy policy and platform terms.

4. Data Retention

Local game saves remain on the device until they are deleted or overwritten.
