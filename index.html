This Privacy Policy explains how Dragon Ace (“the App”) processes user data. Dragon Ace is a VR dragon-riding game for Meta Quest.

1. Data We Process and Why

Meta Account Information

With the required platform permissions, the App uses your Meta User ID and username or display name to identify you, associate leaderboard results with your account, retrieve personal best results and rankings, and display your player name. Your User ID is not publicly displayed in leaderboards.

Game Data

The App processes level progress, game settings, scores, completion times, star ratings, personal best results, and gameplay action results to save progress, display results, provide leaderboards, analyze interaction outcomes, and improve the game experience.

When you first enable the relevant features, we explain what data will be uploaded and why. We submit the relevant data to Meta only after you confirm. This includes leaderboard scores, completion times, and gameplay action results.

VR Interaction Data

The App processes headset and controller positions and orientations, along with hand-tracking data, on your device in real time to display the VR view and support gameplay. The App does not store or actively upload this tracking data. It does not use microphone or eye-tracking data.

2. Information Visible in Leaderboards

The App uses Meta platform services to provide global leaderboards. After you confirm that you want to enable leaderboards, your results are automatically submitted when you complete a level and associated with your platform account.

Other players can view your player name, score, completion time, and rank. Your User ID and gameplay action results are not publicly displayed in leaderboards.

3. Data Storage and Recipients

Game progress and settings are stored on your device. When Meta Cloud Backup is enabled, game saves may also be backed up to Meta’s cloud. Account, leaderboard, and gameplay action records are processed through Meta platform services.

The App does not send this data to self-hosted servers or third-party services other than Meta.

Meta’s processing of relevant data through its platform and SDKs is also subject to the Meta Privacy Policy and applicable platform terms.

4. Data Retention

Local saves remain until deleted or overwritten. You can clear them using the in-game progress reset or save deletion feature.

No fixed automatic deletion period has been set for leaderboard and gameplay action records. We retain these records only while needed for the relevant game features. We delete the relevant records without undue delay when they are no longer needed, after verifying a deletion request, when you delete your Meta account, or when required by Meta or applicable law.

Cloud backups are retained and rotated by Meta’s backup service. Deleting local saves or uninstalling the App does not automatically delete cloud backups, leaderboard records, or gameplay action records. Reinstalling the App may restore backed-up saves.

5. Your Rights and Deletion Requests

You can email ronliu666@gmail.com to ask about our data practices or request access, correction, or deletion of personal data under applicable law.

  • Local saves: use the in-game progress reset or save deletion feature.
  • Leaderboard and gameplay action records: email us at the address above and identify the records you want deleted. We may take reasonable steps to verify your identity and will delete the relevant records without undue delay after verification.
  • Cloud backups: sign in to Meta’s Cloud Backups page and delete the relevant Dragon Ace backups. If backups exist on multiple devices, delete the relevant backups for each device.

Do not send account passwords, access tokens, or other confidential credentials by email.

6. Data Security

We use two-factor authentication for the Meta developer account to help prevent unauthorized access to platform data and services.

7. Age Eligibility

The App is intended for Meta’s Teens and Adults audience: users aged 13 and older, or the applicable age in the user’s region.

If you believe a user below the applicable age has provided personal data through the App, contact us so we can review and address a deletion request.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy to reflect changes to the App or legal requirements and will update the effective date on this page. Where additional notice or consent is required, we will provide that notice or obtain that consent.
